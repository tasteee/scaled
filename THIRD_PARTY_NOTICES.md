# Third-party catalog content

## Scope and provenance

The music catalogs and reference captures were extracted or derived from
Scaler 2 version 2.9.0 during development. This includes source-derived material
in src/data/, saved reference results in tests/fixtures/, and the corresponding
content embedded in compiled JavaScript and source maps. Normalizing a schema,
compacting a table, or converting timing units does not establish a new license
for the underlying content.

The data includes chord and scale definitions, note metadata, factory voicings,
performance patterns and adaptation tables, and ranked chord-transition tables.
The external analysis archive preserves the original captures and extraction
history; it is not a dependency of the published library.
